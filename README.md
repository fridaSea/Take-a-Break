## Refresh Your Mind

###

Your companion for your breaks, whenever and wherever you need a little reset or a fresh mind.

A project with frontend focus.
No backend database.

🔗 [Live Demo](https://refresh-your-mind.netlify.app/)

## ✨ Features

- Check-In with yourself
- Choose a version of yourself that you want to embody
